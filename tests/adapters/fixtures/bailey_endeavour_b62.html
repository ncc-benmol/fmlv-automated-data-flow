Essential Habitation Equipment

Within the quoted MRO figure there is an allowance made for ‘essential habitation equipment’ that consists of the following:

  • 75kg for vehicle driver
  • 90% fuel capacity
  • 10kg for gas bottles

The MRO is calculated with the fresh water tank empty. It is not recommended that you travel with water in the fresh water tank, but if you choose to do so, your available payload will reduce accordingly.
