Pioneer

from £199995.00

from £199995.00

The Pioneer is the ultimate luxury motorhome and can be designed with a single or double slide-out. The initial slide-out contains a well-equipped modern kitchen including all your cooking facilities.

The Pioneer has a fixed island bed as standard which can be upgraded to an additional slide-out to accommodate a queen size bed. The luxurious bedroom is also complete with a generous wardrobe and a separate shower and toilet area, which can be connected to form a spacious, private dressing area from either saloon or bedroom.

The spacious interior is coupled with a substantial cupboard and drawer space throughout.

The Pioneer IB is available with sleeping up to six people in comfort.
