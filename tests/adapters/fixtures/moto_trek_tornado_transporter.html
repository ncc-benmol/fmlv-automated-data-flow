Tornado Transporter

POA

POA

The compact Tornado Transporter is the ideal vehicle to carry up to two competition motorbikes or go-karts with overnight accommodation for up to three people and seat belted seats for up to five.

The living area provides all the essentials for a comfortable overnight stay, with a fixed double bed and dining area.

There is an ample size garage that creates great storage with anti-slip flooring, wall protection and lashing points, which can be positioned to suit. The 3500kg Tornado Transporter can be tailor made to suit.
