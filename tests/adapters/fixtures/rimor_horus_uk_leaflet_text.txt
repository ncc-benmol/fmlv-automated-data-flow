On the road to fun.
Your next adventure with Horus starts here. Do it your way, because there is a Horus for 
every free soul. Compact and sleek or large and spacious, choose the van you prefer from 
a range designed to meet your needs. Some examples? Horus 45, with overlapping and 
modular double beds at the rear, offers up to five comfortable berths in total. Or super-
versatile Horus 40 with the rear area that changes from a large lounge to a comfortable 
sleeping area in just a few simple steps. And Horus 66, with even more generous spaces 
with a surprising lounge area that changes into a storage area.
Control the lights from your smartphone and recharge the auxiliary batteries efficiently 
with the new smart MEB-300 system. Enjoy the comfort of home in all the configurations: 
with double, twin or French beds.  Get ready to be amazed by the new features: from 
the front and side driver’s cabin blinds to the large 700x500 mm panoramic window, as 
standard throughout the range.
