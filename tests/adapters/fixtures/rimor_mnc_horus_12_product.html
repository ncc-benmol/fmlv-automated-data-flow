Rimor Horus 12

The Rimor Horus 12 reaches exceptional levels of affordable quality and style. The all-new Rimor Horus 12 is a low-profile campervan with 3 berths and 4 travelling seats. The Horus 12 offers a rear double French bed that also lifts to create more storage space for travel and a front lounge that converts into a single bed with a wetroom containing a shower, toilet and washbasin. The Rimor Horus 12 is based on the new Euro 6D 2.2L 140BHP Fiat Ducato chassis.
