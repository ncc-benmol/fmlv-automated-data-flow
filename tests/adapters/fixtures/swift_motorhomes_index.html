  • What is the difference between a campervan and motorhome?

    While campervans and motorhomes share some similarities, a campervan is typically a smaller, more compact vehicle that is based on a standard van. A motorhome, on the other hand, is typically a larger vehicle and are built by fitting a coach-built living space on the back of a chassis, and they typically include more amenities and features than a campervan.

    The choice between a campervan and motorhome ultimately depends on your personal preferences, budget, and travel needs.

  • What makes a motorhome family-friendly?

    From seating to sleeping arrangements and bathroom facilities to onboard amenities, there’s plenty to consider when choosing a motorhome to accommodate the needs of both adults and children. Swift offers a range of thoughtfully designed motorhomes with key features such as built in Wi-Fi, zonal heating and integrated garages, creating an enjoyable experience for families of all sizes.

  • Is it legal to live in a motorhome full-time?

    Overall, while it is generally legal to live in a motorhome full time in the UK, it's important to carefully consider your options and obtain the appropriate permissions. Your vehicle will require a valid MOT and comply with relevant laws and regulations related to parking, waste disposal, and safety.

  • Where can I empty my motorhome toilet UK?

    In the UK, there are a number of places where you can legally and safely empty your motorhome toilet, including service stations, campsites and caravan parks, motorhome service points or local authority waste disposal sites.

    It's important to note that dumping chemical toilet waste in non-designated areas, such as public toilets or roadside drains, is illegal and can pose a health and environmental hazard. It's always best to use designated facilities and dispose of waste safely and responsibly.

  • Are motorhomes easy to drive?

    The ease of driving a motorhome depends on various factors, including the vehicle's size, your driving experience, and your comfort level. Smaller motorhomes are generally easier to drive, while larger ones can be more challenging.

    With practice, education, and confidence, many people find that driving a motorhome becomes more comfortable over time.

  • Are motorhomes exempt to a MOT test?

    All motorhomes, including campervans, must undergo an annual MOT test once they are more than three years old. It's important to keep your motorhome well-maintained and serviced throughout the year to help ensure that it passes its MOT test. Failing to have a valid MOT certificate can result in a fine, penalty points on your licence, and even prosecution in some cases.

  • Are motorhomes good value for money?

    Whether or not motorhomes are a good value for money depends on a number of factors, including your individual needs and preferences. The type of motorhome you are considering, your budget, frequency of use, depreciation, maintenance and its resale value are all things to consider when you do your research.

  • Where can you store a motorhome?

    Storing a motorhome when it's not in use is a common practice among owners, especially during the offseason or when you're not on an extended trip. There are several options for motorhome storage, depending on your location, preferences, and budget.
