Using camper vans in everyday life

Every camper van from WEINSBERG is based on a classic box van from Fiat or Mercedes. Extensive conversions turn the box van into a fully‐fledged camping vehicle which, thanks to its special equipment, has earned the name „camper van‟. However, a camper van has not forgotten its capabilities as a versatile transport vehicle. Thanks to the large rear doors and variable storage space, it offers a high level of everyday utility for all those who have a lot to transport. Whether craftsmen, sportsmen or only regular furniture store visitors: With a camper van from WEINSBERG, you receive a powerful holiday vehicle and a practical transporter at the same time. Insider tip: The CaraBus/CaraBus GREY [OUTLAW] with its huge gas‐tight rear garage. Absolutely have a look at it!
