Discover the CaraCore layouts

That the CaraCore carries the WEINSBERG values inside was clear from the beginning. These include family friendliness and usability – this is exactly what is reflected in the three layouts. Whether double beds, single beds or simply the largest bathroom in its class: the CaraCore impresses with flexibility, plenty of space and clever solutions. Like for example the lifting bed which is located above the driver’s cabin and can simply be lowered as required.

On the road with the CaraCore

#teamWEINSBERG

Rudi and Gerlinde

We are already old hands at camping. What is new, however, is our CaraCore, which we are taking out for the first time this year – but properly! Our favourite destination Norway is on the itinerary. Without any time limit, but at a time of the year where more locals than tourists are around. From Bavaria we drive along the fjords up to the Lofoten. Completely according to our own rhythm and with a sense of style. After all, the CaraCore shines with its outstanding design and fits perfectly to Scandinavia.
